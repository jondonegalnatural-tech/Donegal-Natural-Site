About Us

Simple ingredients,
extraordinary love.

Established in 1993 as an independent, family owned and operated wholesale natural canine treat and chew company, Donegal Natural Dog Treats' sole mission is to provide the safest, healthiest and tastiest delectables to our canine family members.

The father/daughter team of Marshall and Morgan put their discerning, experienced eyes on each and every treat and chew that enters and leaves their factory/warehouse. It's a time-consuming, old-fashioned way of doing business — but it's the only way this team knows that a safe, healthy treat has earned its way in front of your loving companion's nose.
